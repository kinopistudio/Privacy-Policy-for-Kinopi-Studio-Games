Kinopi Studio Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile games published by Kinopi Studio.

Effective date: July 19, 2026

Overview

Kinopi Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use our games.

1. Games Covered by This Policy

  • Planet Game

This list may be updated when Kinopi Studio publishes additional games.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features and third-party services used by a game, we and our service providers may collect the following categories of information:

  • Gameplay and usage information: game sessions, progress, gameplay events, feature interactions, advertising events, and related activity.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system, language, app version, IP address, installation identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, crash logs, and diagnostic data.
  • Advertising information: ad impressions, ad interactions, rewarded-ad activity, consent choices, and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Purchase information: purchased products, transaction identifiers, and purchase status when in-app purchases are available. Complete payment-card details are handled by Google Play and are not directly received or stored by Kinopi Studio.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve our games.
  • Understand gameplay and improve the player experience.
  • Measure performance and troubleshoot crashes or technical problems.
  • Display, measure, and manage advertisements.
  • Process and verify in-app purchases.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, cheating, and security incidents.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services that process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Service provider Possible purpose
Unity Technologies Analytics, advertising, app diagnostics, and game services.
Google Play services App distribution, purchases, licensing, and platform functionality.

Third-party services may collect or process information as described in their own privacy documentation. Kinopi Studio is responsible for disclosing the third-party services integrated into each game.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate, analyze, secure, support, distribute, and monetize our games.

We may also disclose information when required by law, in response to valid legal requests, to protect users or our rights, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of a game.

Kinopi Studio does not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

6. Consent and Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may be shown choices to consent to, reject, or limit analytics, personalized advertising, or other data processing. You may also manage advertising preferences through your device settings or privacy controls offered by relevant service providers.

To request access to or deletion of information associated with your use of a Kinopi Studio game, contact us using the email address below. We may need information sufficient to identify the relevant data.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our games, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

Data stored only on your device may be deleted when you clear the game’s data or uninstall the game. Retention periods for information processed by third-party service providers are governed by their respective policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our games are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Data Security

We and our service providers use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. International Data Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have data-protection laws that differ from those in your jurisdiction.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our games, services, or legal obligations change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date at the top.
